.. section:: How does it work?

EkosLive requires an internet connection. A fast and high bandwidth internet connection is recommended since image transfer depends on your internet speed. A low-bandwidth internet for device control is sufficient.

.. image:: /_static/images/ekoslive_block_diagram.jpg
  :width: 400px
  :align: center

Cloud Storage is available for EkosLive Pro users. Captured images (not preview) may be saved to the cloud as lossless compressed FITS images (.fits.gz). Users can view the cloud images and utilize an advanced metadata system to search and categorize their images.
