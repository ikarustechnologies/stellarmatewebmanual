.. section::SM App
StellarMate App communicates with StellarMate controllers over WiFi. By default, StellarMate Controller creates its own Access Point (Hotspot) that you can connect to by joining your Phone/Tablet to the StellarMate hotspot and then scanning for StellarMate devices on the network.

To configure StellarMate network settings, use StellarMate App Device tab to configure WiFi, Frequency, Channel, and Direct Ethernet.
