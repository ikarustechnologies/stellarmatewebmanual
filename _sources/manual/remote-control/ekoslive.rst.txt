.. section::Ekoslive

EkosLive is an online platform to access your StellarMate remotely. It offers simplified web-based controls for Ekos along with advanced features such as Stella AI, Cloud Storage, and Observatory Management. EkosLive Basic subscription is completely free for all StellarMate users.

Once StellarMate is configured via StellarMate App, connect StellarMate to the unit and head over to EkosLive to access it remotely.
You can check To monitor whether **EkosLive** is successfully connected, look for the connection status icons. These icons are represented by **cross icons** that indicate whether the connection is established or not. Ekoslive is connected successfully.

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/connection/ekos-profile.jpg
  :width: 400px
  :align: center

To manage the connection settings, click on the **configuration icon**. This icon allows you to toggle between different connection options and configure your settings as necessary.

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/connection/toggle.jpg
  :width: 400px
  :align: center

Within the configuration settings, you will find a field where you can insert the **URL for EkosLive Online**. Make sure to provide the correct URL that points to your EkosLive instance.

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/connection/show.jpg
  :width: 400px
  :align: center

Once the URL has been entered, press the **Connect** button. Upon successful connection, both **EkosLive Online** and **EkosLive Offline** will establish a connection with **KStars**. This will enable the synchronization between the two platforms.

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/connection/connected.jpg
  :width: 400px
  :align: center
