.. subsection:: Backup and Restore Process

This is an instructional guide for the Backup and Restore process.
It will be explained step by step in this section of the manual.

**Backing up StellarMate configuration**
In order to backup your StellarMate configuration, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the **Backup** button.

.. image:: /_static/images/sm_dashboard_logs.jpeg
  :width: 400px
  :align: center


2. Wait for the Backup process to generate a backup file, this might take long if you have lots of images.

.. image:: /_static/images/sm_dashboard_ekos_logs.jpeg
  :width: 400px
  :align: center


3. Click on the **Download** button (cloud icon) and select the backup file that has been generated.

.. image:: /_static/images/sm_dash_diag_backup_dl_dl.jpg
  :width: 400px
  :align: center

4. You should get a Backup Successful message. You should You now have a backup file on your computer that you can use later to restore your StellarMate configuration.

.. image:: /_static/images/sm_dash_diag_backup_success.jpg
  :width: 400px
  :align: center


**Restoring StellarMate configuration**
In order to restore your StellarMate configuration, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the **Restore** button.

.. image:: /_static/images/sm_dashboard_nm_logs.jpeg
  :width: 400px
  :align: center

2. Select your backup file and click on **Open**.

.. image:: /_static/images/sm_dash_diag_restore_dialoglick.jpg
  :width: 400px
  :align: center

3. Wait for the upload process to complete.

4. You should get a Upload Successful message, which means that the restore was successful.

.. image:: /_static/images/sm_dashboard_boot_logs.jpeg
  :width: 400px
  :align: center
