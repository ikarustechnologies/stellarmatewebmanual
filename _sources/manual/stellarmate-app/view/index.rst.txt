.. section:: View

The View tab displays online images saved on the cloud (requires EkosLive Pro subscription, refer to Resources section) and offline images captured on StellarMate. All metadata is preserved so you can sort and search your images at any time. You can toggle between Cloud or Offline images. You can also switch between Images / Videos.

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/tabs/View/view.jpeg
    :align: center
    :width: 400px
