.. subsection:: Videos

In videos section, you can view folders and files under "/Videos" directory. Directory name and it's is displayed under the folder/file.

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/tabs/View/videos/video.jpg
    :align: center
    :width: 400px

You can press on the select button, once selected press on the files you would like to delete.

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/tabs/View/videos/select.jpg
    :align: center
    :width: 400px

Once the files are selected, you can see the checkmarks on the top of it.

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/tabs/View/videos/selected.jpg
    :align: center
    :width: 400px

Once you confirm deleting the files, selected files are deleted from the filesystem.

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/tabs/View/videos/deleted.jpeg
    :align: center
    :width: 400px
