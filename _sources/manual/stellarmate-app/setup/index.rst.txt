.. section::Setup

Go to your mobile OS WiFi settings and connect to the StellarMate hotspot.
- Hotspot: stellarmate
- Password: stellar@mate

.. note:: Before using the StellarMate App to configure your unit for the first time, please use the networking settings in your iOS/Android phone/tablet to connect to the HotSpot first. Once connected, proceed to run the StellarMate App.

.. image:: /_static/images/stellarmate_wifi_connection.JPG
    :width: 400px
    :align: center


After connecting to StellarMate, go back to the App:

- Tap on RESCAN to start the scanning process. Discovered devices would be listed on the left side pane.

.. image:: /_static/images/Setup/registration/rescan.jpg
    :width: 400px
    :align: center

.. image:: /_static/images/Setup/registration/welcome.jpg
    :width: 400px
    :align: center


- After StellarMate is detected, tap on it to establish connection. Wait for a few seconds and the LED would turn green indicating a stable successful connection.

.. image:: /_static/images/Setup/page-connnecting.jpg
    :width: 400px
    :align: center


The right side pane should now display the Equipment Profile page. Now you are ready to connect to your astronomical equipment.
