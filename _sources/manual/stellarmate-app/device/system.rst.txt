.. subsection:: System

System shows the Utilization of SM device. It includes: 
    * CPU
    * RAM
    * Storage
    * Temperature

It also has main controls i.e

    * Power Off the SM unit
    * Restart SM, you may also see the change of LED light in RPI 5 and SM X
    * Access Web Manager. It can also be accessed from the browser i.e http://localhost:8624
  
.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/tabs/Device/system/web-manager.jpg
    :align: center
    :width: 400px

* Access of VNC. Can be accessed from the browser i.e http://localhost:6080

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/tabs/Device/system/vnc.jpeg
    :align: center
    :width: 400px

You can also update your VNC password by pressinng on the top right **"Update VNC Password"**
