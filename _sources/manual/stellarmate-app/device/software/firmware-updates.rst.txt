.. subsection:: Firmware Updates:level: 1

When there is an update available for StellarMate, an icon will show up on Device tab.

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/tabs/Device/device-update.jpeg
    :align: center
    :width: 400px

To update StellarMate, simply click the download button and wait for the the process to complete.

.. image:: /_static/images/sm-device/sm-tools/device.jpg
    :align: center
    :width: 400px

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/tabs/Device/update.jpeg
    :align: center
    :width: 400px

Once the update is complete, you will be prompted to restart Stellarmate for the updates to take effect.
