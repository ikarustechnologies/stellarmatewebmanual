.. subsection:: hiPs Overlay :level: 1
Toggle Overlay to increase the Hips opacity.
image
It is only available when WCS is enabled, image is plate-solved and once your Camera FOV is 60 arcminutes or more.

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/overlay/overlay.jpg
   :width: 400px
   :align: center

Once preview is captured, you can toggle it and increase/decrease the HiPs opacity.
