Mosaics can be imported from Telescopius online tool. 

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/modules/scheduler/mosaic_planner.png
    :align: center
    :width: 400px

1. **Target:** Name of the target.
2. **CSV:** You can copy coordinates from https://telescopius.com/  and paste it here. Makue sure you include  center coordinates and then copy csv. 
3. **Sequence:** Include sequence from the directory browser.
4. **Steps:** Enable steps to perform.
5. **Import Mosaic:** Imports mosaic sequences.

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/modules/scheduler/mosaic/sequences.jpg
    :align: center
    :width: 400px

Mosaic planner sequences are added here, After importing mosaic.
