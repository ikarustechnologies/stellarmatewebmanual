**Mount Status**

Shows the status of the mount if it is parking/unparking, slewing, tracking.
Displays the current target object name
Right Ascension,
Declination
**Camera Status**

Shows the current status of the camera: 
   **Capturing:** When the camera is busy capturing images.
   **Complete:** Sequence or Image is captured successfully.
   **Idle:** Not capturing any images.
Displays the name of the camera.
Active filter name.

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/statusbar/top/camera-name.jpg
   :align: center
   :width: 400px

Once you press on the filter name, you will be able to edit Filter names.

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/statusbar/top/filter-name.jpg
   :align: center
   :width: 400px

You can also access Capture settings by pressing the Cog wheel button on the top right.

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/statusbar/top/filters.jpg
