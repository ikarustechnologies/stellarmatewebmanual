    :align: center
    :width: 400px

Region of Interest: Specify the video frame. By default, a full frame is captured. All units in pixels

    * **Left:** Left offset
    * **Top:** Top offset
    * **Width:** Width (unbinned)
    * **Height:** Height (unbinned)
Left+Width should not exceed the maximum width of the camera. The same rule applies to Top+Height.
