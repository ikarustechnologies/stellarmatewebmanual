.. subsection:: Preview Framing :level:2

.. warning:: Preview images are not saved in Internal Storage

.. image:: /_static/images/ekos/modules/QuickCameraControls/controls.jpg
     :align: center
     :width: 400px

1) You can capture a preview image by tapping on the Camera button. 
     An animated circular progress icon will show the progress of the current capture and will show you when the image is being downloaded to the app by showing a green cloud download icon.
2) Looping can be done as well by tapping on the Loop button (repeat icon). 


3) You can stop the capturing process by tapping on the Stop button anytime while it's capturing.
