.. section:: Updating StellarMate

Ikarus Technologies releases a new StellarMate update every 2 months. Hot fixes are pushed on demand. To update your unit, the controller must be connected to the internet either via WiFi or Ethernet.

There are two methods to update StellarMate OS:
1. **Via VNC**
Run StellarMate Tools from the desktop. Click Software Updater to begin the update process. It might take a few minutes to complete. Do not restart the unit until the process is complete.

.. image:: /_static/images/sm-device/sm-tools/tools.jpg
  :width: 400px
  :align: center

2. **Via StellarMate App**

Navigate to the Device Tab and then tap Software Update. After the update is complete, please restart the unit

.. image:: /_static/images/sm-device/sm-tools/device.jpg
  :width: 400px
