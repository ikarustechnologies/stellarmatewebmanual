.. section:: WiFi

**Note**
You must first complete the First Time Setup guide before proceeding with this tutorial.



You can connect to StellarMate **wirelessly** either via:

    * Direct Connection to the StellarMate **HotSpot** or
    * Joining StellarMate to your **Home WiFi**.

.. image:: /_static/images/hotspot_wifi_mode.jpg
