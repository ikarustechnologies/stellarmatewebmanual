   {\LARGE\bfseries Introduction\par}
   \vspace{1em}

Welcome to StellarMate! Thank you for choosing our revolutionary astrophotography platform that combines powerful open-source technology with user-friendly operation. Whether you're capturing your first lunar image or setting up a remote observatory, this manual will guide you through your astronomical imaging journey.

.. raw:: latex

   {\Large\bfseries Core Features\par}
   \vspace{0.5em}

StellarMate integrates industry-leading open-source software to provide a comprehensive astrophotography solution:

* **Complete Equipment Control** : Direct operation of telescopes, cameras, filter wheels, and focusers
* **Advanced Imaging** : Automated plate solving, focusing, guiding, and image sequencing
* **Cross-Platform Support** : Available on iOS, Android, Windows, MacOS, and Linux
* **Remote Operation** : Built-in WiFi hotspot and wired connectivity options

.. raw:: latex

   {\Large\bfseries Key Components\par}
   \vspace{0.5em}

The StellarMate ecosystem consists of three main elements:

* **Hardware** : A compact, powerful control unit for your astronomical equipment
* **Mobile App** : Intuitive control via iOS and Android devices
* **Desktop Software** : Full-featured control through KStars/EKOS

This manual covers everything from basic setup to advanced imaging techniques, helping you make the most of your StellarMate device. Each section provides clear, step-by-step instructions and helpful tips for successful astrophotography.
